# Credits

## UI emoji icons

The UI emojis use SVG icons from the sources below, rasterized to white
transparent 128px PNGs (stored in `emojis-ui/`).

- **Bootstrap Icons** — <https://github.com/twbs/icons>  
  Copyright (c) 2019-2024 The Bootstrap Authors. Licensed under the MIT License.  
  Used for `boss`, `timer`, `mvp`, `duo`, `retry`, `gift`, `check`, `cross`,
  `settings`, `surrender`, `stun`, `strength`, `weakness`, `vulnerable`,
  `regeneration`, `hemorrhage`, `glitch`, `haste`, `atropa` (flower2) and `quest`.
- **Lucide** (`crown`, `sparkles`, `battery-charging`, `drama`, `paw-print`,
  `skull`) — <https://lucide.dev>  
  Copyright (c) 2022 Lucide Contributors. Licensed under the ISC License.  
  `drama` is used for `masks`, `paw-print` for `creatures`, and `skull` for the
  defeats counter.
- The small `dot` marker is a custom-drawn white circle.

The Veeronica and Jane Doe character icons use the Damnation Wiki NavIcons
(wikitide.net). The Sk8, Activate
Battery, Crystal Pitch, Purified and Hatchet ability icons are from the
[Forsaken Wiki](https://forsaken.wiki). The Noli Observant ability icon, the
Rejuvenate the Rotten ability icon and the rotten minion render are from the
[Forsaken Wiki on Fandom](https://forsaken2024.fandom.com/wiki/Noli).
